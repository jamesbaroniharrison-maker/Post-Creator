====================================================================
  WPA CONTENT ENGINE - HOW TO USE
====================================================================

STARTING THE APP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Find the file called "Start WPA Content Engine.bat" in this same
   folder.
   TIP: right-click it and choose "Send to > Desktop (create
   shortcut)" so you have an icon on your desktop and never need to
   find this folder again.
2. Double-click it.
3. A black window will pop up and show some text scrolling by. This
   is normal - just leave it alone. The first time you do this it
   can take a minute or two.
4. Once it's ready, your web browser will open automatically to the
   login page. If it doesn't open after a couple of minutes, look at
   the black window for a line that says
        App running at: http://localhost:....
   and type that address into your browser yourself.

USING THE DASHBOARD
---------------------------------------------------------------------
When you log in you'll see, from top to bottom:

STATS
  A quick scoreboard - how many posts are drafted, approved,
  published, or rejected, and how quickly you're getting through
  them.

QUICK ACTIONS
  Three shortcuts:
    - "Generate a post now" - type in a topic, pick a post type, and
      click "Research + draft". It'll research the topic and write a
      draft post in your voice, usually within a minute.
    - "Run research now" - normally the system checks for news once
      a day by itself. This button makes it check right now instead
      of waiting.
    - "Force next search topics" - if there's something specific you
      want it to look for next time it checks the news (e.g. "WPA
      dental cover"), type it in here and click "Queue topic". It'll
      be included the next time the research runs, then it clears
      itself automatically.

WEEKLY INPUT
  This is for your own material - photos, voice notes, or just a
  quick typed note about something on your mind. Pick a post type,
  either type a note and click "Draft from note", or drag a photo/
  audio file into the box and click "Upload & draft". Videos aren't
  supported - only photos and audio.

THIS WEEK'S DRAFTS
  Every draft waiting for your attention, grouped by the day it's
  suggested for. For each one you can:
    - Edit the text directly - just click into the box and type.
      It saves automatically when you click away.
    - Add or remove hashtags and tags - type into the small box and
      click "Add", or click the x on an existing one to remove it.
    - Change the suggested day using the dropdown.
    - Click APPROVE if you're happy with it, or REJECT and pick a
      reason if you're not.
    - Once approved, click "Copy text" to copy the post, paste it
      into LinkedIn yourself, and once it's actually posted, click
      "Mark published".
    - Once published, you can come back later and type in the likes
      and comments it got, for your own records.

  Nothing is EVER posted to LinkedIn automatically. You always copy
  and paste it in yourself. This system never has the ability to
  post on your behalf.

TOPIC BANK
  Ideas the system has already found and scored, that haven't turned
  into a draft yet. Click "Generate draft" on any of them to turn it
  into a real post.

CLOSING THE APP
---------------------------------------------------------------------
When you're done, you can just close the browser tab. If you also
want to shut down the black "SERVER" window, that's fine too - it'll
just mean the app isn't reachable again until you double-click the
starter file next time.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
---------------------------------------------------------------------
- If a page looks broken or shows an error message, try refreshing
  it first.
- If that doesn't help, close everything (the browser tab and the
  black SERVER window) and double-click the starter file again.
- If it still doesn't work, take a screenshot of whatever error
  you're seeing and send it to James.
