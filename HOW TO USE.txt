====================================================================
  LINKEDIN CONTENT ENGINE - YOUR GUIDE
====================================================================
This covers everything: starting the app and using every part of it
day to day.

====================================================================
PART 1 - STARTING THE APP
====================================================================
1. Find the file called "Start Content Engine.bat" in the project
   folder (or the shortcut on your desktop if you made one).
   TIP: right-click it and choose "Send to > Desktop (create
   shortcut)" so you always have an icon and never need to hunt for
   the folder.
2. Double-click it.
3. A black window will pop up and show some text scrolling by. This
   is normal - just leave it alone. The first time you do this it
   can take a minute or two.
4. Once it's ready, your web browser will open automatically to the
   login page. If it doesn't open after a couple of minutes, look at
   the black window for a line that says
        App running at: http://localhost:....
   and type that address into your browser yourself.

LOGGING IN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Use the username and password you set up. If you forget the
password, there's no "forgot password" link (single-user system) -
it needs a direct database fix.

Login came back once remote access (via Tailscale, so you can reach
the dashboard from your phone) meant this app is no longer guaranteed
to only be reachable from this one machine.

====================================================================
PART 2 - THE PAGES
====================================================================
Along the top of every page is a menu: Home, Review, Accepted,
Rejected, Topic Bank, Past Weeks, Settings. Click any of them to jump
straight there.

HOME
  A quick scoreboard (how many posts are drafted, approved, published,
  rejected) and the "Weekly input" box for your own material - photos,
  voice notes, or just a quick typed note about something on your
  mind. Pick a post type, either type a note and click "Draft from
  note", or drag a photo/audio file in and click "Upload & draft".
  Videos aren't supported - only photos and audio.

REVIEW
  Every draft waiting for a decision, grouped by the day it's
  suggested for. Each card also shows the content-framework tags for
  that post (funnel stage, hook style, length, format, media pairing)
  so you can see why it's shaped the way it is. For each one you can:
    - Edit the text directly - click into the box and type. It saves
      automatically when you click away.
    - Add or remove hashtags and tags - type into the small box and
      click "Add", or click the x on an existing one to remove it.
    - Change the suggested day using the dropdown.
    - Click ACCEPT if you're happy with it.
    - Click REDRAFT if the topic's right but you want the system to
      have another go at writing it - the old version moves to
      Rejected and a fresh one appears here.
    - Click REJECT and pick a reason if you don't want it at all.

  Nothing is EVER posted to LinkedIn automatically. You always copy
  and paste it in yourself. This system never has the ability to post
  on your behalf.

ACCEPTED
  Everything you've accepted, automatically sorted into weeks (up to
  4 posts a week - if you accept more than that, the extras roll into
  the following week by themselves, no need to manage this yourself).

  Near the top you'll see two rows of buttons:
    - A week selector (This week / Next week / and two more ahead) -
      click through to look at what's coming up.
    - A filter (All / Accepted, not published / Published) - handy
      when you're logging likes and comments and don't want to wade
      through posts that aren't live yet.

  For each post:
    - You can still edit the text, hashtags, tags, and compliance note
      here, even after it's been marked published - nothing is ever
      locked. Click into a box, type, click away, it saves.
    - Click "Copy text" to copy it, then paste into LinkedIn yourself.
    - Once it's actually posted, click "Mark published".
    - Once published, come back later and type in the likes and
      comments it got, for your own records.

  Further down is "Plan ahead" - a full month laid out one week at a
  time, top to bottom (not just the current week). For any day, if you
  already know it should be about something specific (a project
  milestone, an event, whatever), type a quick note and save it. When
  you're ready, click "Generate draft" on that day and it'll write the
  post using your note as the brief.

  Each week also has its own "Fill this week" button. If a week's
  gone quiet and it hasn't reached the usual 3-4 posts, click it and
  the system drafts however many more it needs - pulling from the
  topic bank first, or a rotating personal-reflection prompt if the
  bank's empty - so a thin week never just goes uncovered. New drafts
  always land in Review first, same as everything else.

REJECTED
  Only the 5 most recent rejected posts are kept - anything older is
  deleted automatically, so this list never gets cluttered. You can
  still click "Reuse as new post" on any of them to have another go.

TOPIC BANK
  Ideas the system has already found and scored (AI news or broader
  market news), that haven't turned into a draft yet. Click "Generate
  draft" on any of them to turn it into a real post.

PAST WEEKS
  Everything from the last 6 weeks, whatever happened to it -
  published, rejected, or just never got to. If you spot a week that
  looks thin, click "Reuse as new post" on anything in there to copy
  it into Settings' Quick Actions, where you can tweak it and generate
  a fresh draft.

SETTINGS
  Quick Actions:
    - "Generate a post now" - type in a topic, pick a post type, and
      click "Research + draft". It'll research the topic and write a
      draft post in your voice, usually within a minute or two.
    - "Run research now" - normally the system checks for news once a
      day by itself. This button makes it check right now instead of
      waiting.
    - "Force next search topics" - if there's something specific you
      want it to look for next time it checks the news, type it in
      and click "Queue topic". It's included next time the research
      runs, then clears itself automatically.

  Email Reminders - fully adjustable, day and time, independently for
  each:
    - Personal story reminder - a nudge to send in a story. If you've
      already sent one in that week, this email changes automatically
      to a quick "it's waiting for you in Review" nudge instead of
      asking for a new one - it won't ask twice.
    - Weekly post digest - a summary of everything lined up that week.
    Set your email address, pick a day and time for each, click
    "Save".

====================================================================
PART 3 - CLOSING THE APP
====================================================================
When you're done, you can just close the browser tab. If you also
want to shut down the black "SERVER" window, that's fine too - it'll
just mean the app isn't reachable again until you double-click the
starter file next time.

====================================================================
PART 4 - IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
====================================================================
- If a page looks broken or shows an error message, try refreshing
  it first.
- If that doesn't help, close everything (the browser tab and the
  black SERVER window) and double-click the starter file again.
- If drafting fails, check that Ollama is actually running
  (`ollama list` in a terminal should show at least one model - if
  it's empty, run `ollama pull llama3` and try again).
